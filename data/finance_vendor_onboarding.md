# Vendor Onboarding Process

## 1. When this applies
Any new vendor expected to receive payment from the company.

## 2. Steps
1. **Request:** the business owner submits a New Vendor Request in the procurement portal, including business justification and estimated annual spend.
2. **Documentation:** the vendor provides a W-9 (or W-8 for non-U.S. vendors), certificate of insurance, and banking details.
3. **Security review:** required for any vendor that will access company systems or handle company or customer data. Contact IT Security.
4. **Approval:** approval level depends on spend. See the Procurement Approval Matrix for thresholds and approvers.
5. **Setup:** Accounts Payable creates the vendor record after approval and verifies bank details by call-back to a known phone number.
6. **PO issued:** a purchase order is issued before work begins for any spend over $2,500.

## 3. Timeline
Standard onboarding takes 5-7 business days once documentation is complete.

## 4. Common delays
Missing W-9, unsigned contract, or an incomplete security questionnaire.
