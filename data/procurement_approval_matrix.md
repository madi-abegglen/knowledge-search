# Procurement Approval Matrix

Approvals are based on total contract value (annual spend for recurring contracts).

| Total value | Required approver(s) |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | Budget owner |
| $2,501 - $10,000 | Department head |
| **$10,001 - $50,000** | **Department head + Finance Director** |
| $50,001 - $250,000 | VP of Finance + COO |
| Over $250,000 | CFO + CEO |

## Rules
- Purchases may not be split to avoid a threshold.
- Sole-source purchases over $10,000 require a written justification.
- Contracts with auto-renewal clauses require Legal review regardless of value.
- Emergency purchases may be approved verbally by the department head, with written approval within 2 business days.
