# Sales Playbook (Sales team)

## Sales stages
1. **Qualify:** confirm budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT).
2. **Discover:** at least one discovery call; document pain points in the CRM.
3. **Demo:** tailored to the buyer's top two use cases.
4. **Proposal:** use the approved template; discounts over 15% need Sales Director approval.
5. **Negotiate and close:** Legal reviews any non-standard terms.

## Discount authority
- Up to 10%: account executive
- 11-15%: sales manager
- Over 15%: Sales Director and Finance

## CRM hygiene
Update opportunity stage and next step within 24 hours of every customer interaction. Opportunities with no activity for 30 days are flagged for review.

## Competitive notes
Do not make disparaging claims about competitors. Use approved battlecards from the sales enablement site.
