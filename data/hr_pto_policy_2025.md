# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy, 2025

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how paid time off is earned and used at Northwind Co.

## 2. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible from their first day. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro-rata.

## 3. Annual PTO allotment
- New hires (0-2 years of service): **20 days per year**
- 3-5 years of service: 25 days per year
- 6+ years of service: 30 days per year

PTO accrues per pay period and is available as it is earned.

## 4. Requesting PTO
Submit requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for absences of 3 days or more. Your manager responds within 3 business days.

## 5. Carryover
Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year. Unused days beyond that are forfeited on January 31.

## 6. Payout
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out at termination where required by state law.

## 7. Related policies
Company holidays are listed in the Holiday Calendar. Extended leave is covered by the Sabbatical Program and the Parental Leave Policy.
