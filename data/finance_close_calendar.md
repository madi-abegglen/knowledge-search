# Month-End Close Calendar (Finance team)

| Business day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| BD1 | Cut-off; AP and AR subledgers closed | AP/AR leads |
| BD2 | Accruals and payroll journal entries posted | GL accountant |
| BD3 | Bank and intercompany reconciliations | Staff accountants |
| BD4 | Revenue recognition review | Revenue accountant |
| BD5 | Flux analysis (variances over 10% or $25k) | Financial analyst |
| BD6 | Controller review | Controller |
| BD7 | Books locked; reporting package issued | FP&A |

## Notes
- Late journal entries after BD3 require Controller approval.
- Reconciling items older than 60 days must be escalated.
