# Company Holidays, 2026

The company observes 11 paid holidays. These do not count against PTO.

| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| Jan 1 | New Year's Day |
| Jan 19 | Martin Luther King Jr. Day |
| May 25 | Memorial Day |
| Jun 19 | Juneteenth |
| Jul 3 | Independence Day (observed) |
| Sep 7 | Labor Day |
| Nov 26 | Thanksgiving Day |
| Nov 27 | Day after Thanksgiving |
| Dec 24 | Christmas Eve |
| Dec 25 | Christmas Day |
| Dec 31 | New Year's Eve |

## Notes
- Employees required to work on a holiday receive a floating day off within 60 days.
- For vacation time beyond company holidays, see the PTO Policy.
