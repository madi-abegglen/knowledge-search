# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Work arrangements
- **Hybrid (default):** 3 days per week in the office, 2 remote. Anchor days are Tuesday and Wednesday.
- **Fully remote:** available for roles approved by the department head and HR.
- **On-site:** required for roles that need physical presence.

## 2. Working from another state or country
Working from a different state for more than 14 days per year requires HR approval (tax and payroll implications). Working from another country requires HR and Legal approval, and is generally limited to 30 days per year.

## 3. Equipment and stipend
A one-time $500 home-office stipend is available. The company provides a laptop and monitor.

## 4. Security expectations
Use the company VPN on public networks, keep devices locked, and do not work from shared computers.

## 5. Core hours
Be reachable between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in your local time zone.
