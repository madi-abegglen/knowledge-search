# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Submitting expenses
Submit expenses in the expense tool within 30 days of purchase, with itemized receipts for any expense over $25.

## 2. Limits
- Meals while traveling: $75 per day
- Client meals: $100 per person, with attendee names listed
- Lodging: up to $250 per night (higher in designated cities with pre-approval)
- Ground transportation: rideshare and taxis are allowed; rental cars require manager approval

## 3. Not reimbursable
Alcohol (unless with a client and pre-approved), personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades not pre-approved.

## 4. Reimbursement timing
Approved expenses are paid on the next bi-weekly payroll run.

Contact Accounts Payable at ap@northwind.example.
