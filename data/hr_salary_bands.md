# Salary Bands (CONFIDENTIAL: HR only)

For use by HR business partners when making offers and conducting calibration. Do not share outside HR.

| Level | Title examples | Band minimum | Band midpoint | Band maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L3 | Analyst, Coordinator | $58,000 | $68,000 | $78,000 |
| L4 | Senior Analyst, Specialist | $74,000 | $87,000 | $100,000 |
| L5 | Manager, Lead | $95,000 | $112,000 | $129,000 |
| L6 | Senior Manager | $120,000 | $140,000 | $160,000 |

## Guidelines
- Offers above the 75th percentile of a band require HR director approval.
- Annual merit increases are budgeted at 3-4% of payroll.
- Do not disclose individual salaries to employees outside the reporting chain.
