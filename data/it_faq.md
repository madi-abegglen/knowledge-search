# IT Help Desk FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to the self-service portal and choose "Forgot password." You will verify with multi-factor authentication. Passwords expire every 180 days and must be at least 14 characters.

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the company VPN client from the Software Center, sign in with your company email, and approve the MFA prompt. The VPN is required when working on public Wi-Fi.

## How do I request new software?
Submit a Software Request in the IT portal. Pre-approved tools are installed within 1 business day; other requests need manager and security approval.

## My laptop is slow or broken. What do I do?
Open a ticket in the IT portal. Hardware issues are handled within 2 business days; loaner laptops are available for urgent cases.

## Can I use personal devices for work email?
Yes, if your device is enrolled in mobile device management (MDM). Enroll via the Company Portal app.

## Help desk hours
Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM Central. After hours, call the on-call line for critical outages only.
