# Security Incident Reporting

## What counts as an incident
Phishing emails, lost or stolen devices, suspected malware, accidental data sharing, or any unauthorized access to company systems.

## What to do
1. **Report immediately** (within 1 hour) using the "Report Phish" button in your email client, or email security@northwind.example.
2. **Do not** click links, open attachments, or forward the suspicious message.
3. **Lost or stolen device:** call the IT on-call line right away so the device can be remotely wiped.
4. **Preserve evidence:** do not delete the message or reboot the device unless Security instructs you to.

## What happens next
Security triages within 1 hour for high-severity reports and within 1 business day for others. You will receive a follow-up when the report is resolved.

## No blame
Prompt reporting is always appreciated, even if you have already clicked a link.
