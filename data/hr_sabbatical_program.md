# Sabbatical Program

## 1. Overview
After 7 years of continuous service, employees may take a paid sabbatical of up to 4 weeks, in addition to regular PTO. A sabbatical is intended for extended rest, study, or personal projects rather than for ordinary vacation.

## 2. Eligibility
- 7+ years of continuous service
- Good standing at the time of application
- One sabbatical per 7-year period

## 3. How to apply
Submit a request to your manager and HR at least 90 days before the proposed start date.

## 4. Pay and benefits
Base pay and benefits continue unchanged during the sabbatical.

## 5. Related
For standard vacation days, see the PTO Policy. For company-wide closures, see the Holiday Calendar.
