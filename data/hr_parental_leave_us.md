# Parental Leave Policy (United States only)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to employees **based in the United States**. Employees outside the U.S. should consult their local HR representative; this document does not describe leave entitlements in other countries.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees with at least 6 months of service.

## 3. Paid leave
- Birth parents: 16 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base pay
- Non-birth parents and adoptive/foster parents: 12 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base pay

## 4. Timing
Leave may begin up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or placement date and must be completed within 12 months of the event.

## 5. Notice
Provide at least 30 days' notice where possible, using the HR portal.

## 6. Returning to work
A phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks) can be arranged with your manager and HR.
