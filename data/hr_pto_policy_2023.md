# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy, 2023

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how paid time off is earned and used at Northwind Co.

## 2. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible after a 90-day introductory period.

## 3. Annual PTO allotment
- New hires (0-2 years of service): **15 days per year**
- 3-5 years of service: 20 days per year
- 6+ years of service: 25 days per year

## 4. Requesting PTO
Submit requests by email to your manager at least 1 week ahead.

## 5. Carryover
Up to 3 unused days carry over into the next calendar year.

## 6. Payout
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out at termination where required by state law.
