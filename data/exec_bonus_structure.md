# Executive Bonus Structure (CONFIDENTIAL: Executive team only)

## 1. Target bonus
- CEO: 100% of base salary
- C-suite (CFO, COO, CTO, CRO): 60% of base salary
- VPs: 35% of base salary

## 2. Payout components
- 60% company revenue and operating-margin targets
- 25% individual strategic objectives
- 15% team engagement score

## 3. Payout range
Payouts range from 0% to 150% of target depending on performance against plan.

## 4. Clawback
Bonuses paid on restated financials are subject to clawback for 24 months.

## 5. Approval
The Compensation Committee of the Board approves all executive payouts each February.
